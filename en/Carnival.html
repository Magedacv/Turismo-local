CARNIVALS OF BOGOTÁ

Carnivals of Bogotá

Carnivals bring together different artistic, musical, and cultural expressions in spaces where people participate in parades, performances, and activities filled with color and tradition.

Bogotá hosts different carnival-style activities that allow people to enjoy parades, music, costumes, and cultural expressions from different regions of Colombia.

Carnivals and cultural expressions of Bogotá
CARNIVAL AND TRADITION

Bogotá Carnival

Bogotá's carnival celebrations bring together different artistic and cultural expressions that use music, dance, costumes, and parades as forms of celebration.

These gatherings allow different traditions and cultural expressions to be represented through parades and performances that bring together artists, cultural groups, and city residents.

Carnivals create spaces where different cultural expressions can come together and be enjoyed by people of different ages.

Experience in Bogotá

You can enjoy parades, performances, music, and cultural presentations during the city's carnival activities.

FAMILY CELEBRATION

Children's Carnivals

Children's carnivals allow kids to participate in cultural activities through costumes, games, music, and performances specially prepared for families.

These celebrations may include costume parades, children's groups, musical shows, and recreational activities in different parts of the city.

Children's participation allows them to discover different cultural expressions while sharing these activities with their families and other residents of Bogotá.

Experience in Bogotá

You can find children's activities, parades, and performances to enjoy carnivals with your family.

PARADES AND EXPRESSIONS

Parades and performances

Parades and performance groups are some of the main expressions of carnival celebrations. Participants use costumes, music, dance, and visual elements to create collective performances.

Parades allow these groups to move through different spaces while presenting choreographies, characters, and representations inspired by different cultural traditions.

These activities turn streets and public spaces into meeting places where spectators can enjoy the performances and take part in the celebration.

Experience in Bogotá

You can watch parades and performance groups during different carnival events held in public spaces throughout Bogotá.
