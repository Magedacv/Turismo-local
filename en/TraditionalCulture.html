TRADITIONS OF BOGOTÁ

Traditional Culture of Bogotá

Bogotá's traditional culture brings together different traditions, customs, and expressions that are part of the identity of its inhabitants. These manifestations reflect the city's history and cultural influences.

Its expressions include family customs, music, dances, popular arts, and different traditions that continue to be present in everyday life and celebrations throughout Bogotá.

Traditions and cultural expressions of Bogotá
BOGOTÁ TRADITIONS

Traditions and customs

Bogotá preserves different customs that are part of the daily lives of its inhabitants. Some of them are related to family gatherings, traditional foods, and activities carried out during weekends.

A common tradition is gathering with family to share typical dishes such as ajiaco and tamal, especially during weekends and different celebrations. These customs help maintain connections between generations.

Walks through traditional neighborhoods, visits to local markets, and family gatherings are also part of Bogotá's culture and help preserve some of the city's customs.

Experience in Bogotá

You can experience these traditions by sharing with local families, visiting traditional markets, and exploring historic neighborhoods of the city.

MUSIC AND DANCE

Traditional music and dances

Music and dance are part of the cultural expressions that have accompanied Bogotá's history. The city has received influences from different regions of Colombia, which can be seen in its musical traditions.

Traditional rhythms from the Andean region include bambuco, pasillo, and guabina. These expressions can be found in cultural performances, festivals, and different artistic activities.

Traditional music also remains present through folk groups and cultural spaces where dances and musical compositions are performed, allowing people to discover regional traditions.

Experience in Bogotá

You can enjoy traditional music and dances at festivals, cultural performances, and spaces dedicated to Colombian folklore.

POPULAR ARTS AND EXPRESSIONS

Popular arts and expressions

Popular arts are part of the cultural expressions found in Bogotá. The city brings together different forms of artistic creation related to its traditions and the identity of its inhabitants.

Examples include handicrafts, ceramics, textiles, and different decorative objects made by artisans. These pieces can represent elements of Colombian and regional culture.

These expressions are also connected to streets, markets, and cultural spaces where artists and artisans share their work and preserve knowledge passed down through generations.

Experience in Bogotá

You can discover these expressions in markets, craft fairs, and cultural spaces where different traditional works are displayed.
