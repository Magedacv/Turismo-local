DISCOVER THE CENTER OF BOGOTÁ

Tourism in the city center of Bogotá

The center of Bogotá brings together some of the city's most representative places, combining history, architecture, art, and tradition in a single journey. Its streets allow visitors to discover different stages of the capital's history while exploring squares, museums, churches, and traditional neighborhoods.

Exploring this area allows visitors to discover iconic places such as La Candelaria, Plaza de Bolívar, and the Gold Museum, while also enjoying its historic streets, restaurants, cafés, and different cultural expressions found in the heart of Bogotá.

Historic center of Bogotá
HISTORIC SITE

La Candelaria

La Candelaria is one of the most important historic areas of Bogotá and is characterized by its streets, colonial houses, and colorful facades. Its surroundings are home to different cultural spaces and traditional establishments.

Walking through its streets allows visitors to learn about part of Bogotá's history while enjoying an area where architecture, art, and different expressions of local culture come together.

Its historic atmosphere makes this area one of the most representative routes for those who want to discover the traditional center of the capital.

Experience in Bogotá

You can walk through its streets and visit its different museums, squares, churches, cafés, and cultural spaces.

ICONIC LANDMARK

Plaza de Bolívar

Plaza de Bolívar is one of the most important public spaces in central Bogotá. Surrounding it are representative buildings such as the Primatial Cathedral, the National Capitol, and the Palace of Justice.

The square serves as a meeting point for visitors and residents of the city, as well as a setting for different cultural, social, and public events throughout the year.

Visiting this space allows visitors to appreciate some of the most important buildings in the historic center and understand their significance within the city.

Experience in Bogotá

It is an ideal starting point for exploring the historic center and visiting the different attractions located nearby.

CULTURAL EXPERIENCE

Gold Museum

The Gold Museum is one of Bogotá's most recognized cultural spaces and houses an important collection of objects made from precious metals. Its exhibitions allow visitors to learn about different aspects of Indigenous societies in Colombia.

The museum's galleries present objects related to the traditions, beliefs, and ways of life of different communities that inhabited Colombian territory before the arrival of the Spanish.

Visiting the museum complements a tour of the historic center with an experience dedicated to the history and cultural heritage of Colombia.

Experience in Bogotá

It is located in the city center and can be visited as part of a cultural tour alongside other historic places in Bogotá.
