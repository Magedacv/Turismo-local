DISCOVER THE SURROUNDINGS OF BOGOTÁ

Tourism around Bogotá

The surroundings of Bogotá offer a wide variety of destinations where nature, history, culture, and adventure come together just a few kilometers from the capital. These places allow visitors to discover the landscapes of the Bogotá savanna and explore different municipalities of Cundinamarca.

Among the most representative destinations are Zipaquirá, Guatavita, and Chía, each offering different experiences for those who want to leave the city and explore the landscapes, traditions, and tourist attractions of the region.

Landscapes around Bogotá
TOURIST DESTINATION

Zipaquirá

Zipaquirá is one of the best-known tourist destinations around Bogotá. The municipality preserves traditional architecture and has different cultural and historical spaces that allow visitors to learn about part of the region's identity.

Its main attraction is the Salt Cathedral, built inside an old salt mine. The tour allows visitors to discover different underground spaces and learn about the historical importance of salt mining in this area of Cundinamarca.

Visiting Zipaquirá allows visitors to combine a tour of the municipality with a cultural and architectural experience different from the attractions found within Bogotá.

Experience near Bogotá

You can explore the historic center of Zipaquirá, visit the Salt Cathedral and discover its history, enjoying a cultural day trip from Bogotá.

NATURE AND CULTURE

Guatavita

Guatavita is a municipality surrounded by natural landscapes and mountains of the Andean region. Its architecture and peaceful atmosphere make it an attractive destination for those who want to explore the surroundings of Bogotá.

One of its main attractions is the Guatavita Lagoon, associated with the traditional legend of El Dorado. The site allows visitors to enjoy a natural environment while learning about some of the stories and traditions associated with the region.

A visit to Guatavita combines nature, history, and culture, offering a different experience for those who want to get away from the pace of the capital.

Experience near Bogotá

You can visit the town of Guatavita and explore its surroundings to discover its landscapes and natural attractions.

CULTURAL DESTINATION

Chía

Chía is one of the municipalities closest to Bogotá and offers a combination of natural, cultural, and gastronomic spaces. Its location in the savanna makes it possible to take a short trip from the capital and enjoy an atmosphere different from the city.

The municipality offers different places to walk, enjoy restaurants, and explore spaces related to the culture of the region. It also preserves elements of its history, architecture, and local traditions that are part of Chía's cultural identity.

Visiting Chía allows visitors to enjoy a quieter experience outside Bogotá, combining gastronomy, culture, and landscapes characteristic of the savanna.

Experience near Bogotá

It is a good option for a short getaway from Bogotá, offering gastronomic, cultural, and natural spaces to explore.
