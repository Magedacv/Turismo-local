FLAVORS OF BOGOTÁ

Traditional cuisine of Bogotá

Bogotá's traditional cuisine reflects the culinary customs of the Andean region and forms part of the city's cultural identity, characterized by hot, comforting, and hearty dishes ideal for its cool mountain climate. This cuisine combines Indigenous and Spanish heritage through slow cooking methods and the use of local ingredients such as corn and native potatoes, giving rise to iconic preparations such as Ajiaco Santafereño, puchero, and hot chocolate with melted cheese.

By discovering these traditional recipes and their ingredients, visitors can enjoy unique gastronomic experiences ranging from historic establishments in the historic center and traditional market squares to fine dining proposals in exclusive areas.

Traditional Bogotá cuisine
TRADITIONAL DISH

Ajiaco Santafereño

Ajiaco Santafereño is one of the most representative dishes of Bogotá. It is a thick soup prepared mainly with different varieties of potatoes, corn, and chicken, traditionally served with cream and capers.

One of its main characteristics is the use of guascas, an aromatic herb that provides the characteristic flavor of this preparation. The dish is usually served hot and is especially representative of the cuisine of the Andean region.

Trying ajiaco allows visitors to experience one of Bogotá's most recognized gastronomic traditions and discover a preparation that forms part of the city's culinary identity.

Experience in Bogotá

To enjoy the complete experience, this dish is best enjoyed in La Candelaria or Bogotá's traditional market squares, where it is served in black clay dishes that retain its heat and reflect Bogotá's traditional customs.

TRADITIONAL DISH

Santafereño Tamal

The Santafereño tamal is a traditional preparation mainly made with corn dough and different ingredients that are cooked wrapped in leaves. Its preparation and ingredients may vary according to the region and family tradition.

In Bogotá, it is commonly served with Santafereño hot chocolate. This combination is part of the city's traditional gastronomic customs and is especially enjoyed during breakfast.

The experience of trying tamal with hot chocolate allows visitors to discover one of the most characteristic combinations of Bogotá's cuisine and enjoy a tradition that continues to be present throughout the city.

Experience in Bogotá

This combination is the quintessential Bogotá breakfast, a weekend ritual that brings together the flavor of tamal with traditional hot chocolate. It can easily be found in traditional restaurants, bakeries, and busy market stalls.

TRADITIONAL DISH

Santafereño Puchero

Santafereño puchero is a traditional preparation from Bogotá's cuisine that combines different meats, tubers, and vegetables. Its cooking process integrates the flavors of its ingredients into a dish representative of the traditional cuisine of the region.

This dish is characterized by being hearty and bringing together different products historically used in the cuisine of the region. Its preparation represents part of the culinary traditions that have developed in Bogotá and its surrounding areas.

Discovering and trying puchero allows visitors to experience a preparation that is less internationally known, but remains important within the traditional gastronomy of the city.

Experience in Bogotá

As it is such a generous dish, it is the perfect lunch to share with family. This experience can be found in traditional restaurants in Bogotá and in gastronomic areas such as Usaquén, where culinary traditions are preserved.
