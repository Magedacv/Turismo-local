SWEET FLAVORS OF BOGOTÁ

Sweets and desserts of Bogotá

Bogotá's sweet tradition is the perfect complement to its cold afternoons, characterized by comforting and handcrafted desserts that combine fresh dairy products, local fruits, and recipes inherited from the colonial era. Among its most treasured specialties are postre de natas, the classic cuajada con melao made with panela, and figs with arequipe and cheese.

This rich confectionery heritage can be experienced in its most authentic form by trying the iconic street obleas in the city's central squares, or by visiting traditional candy shops and tea rooms in historic neighborhoods.

Traditional Bogotá sweets
TRADITIONAL DESSERT

Postre de Natas

Postre de natas is a gem of Bogotá's traditional confectionery. It is carefully prepared by collecting the layers of cream that form when milk is boiled, which are then cooked in a sweet syrup with raisins. This smooth and creamy dessert preserves a traditional preparation that forms part of the city's confectionery heritage.

It is traditionally enjoyed in pastry shops in the historic center and in local cuisine restaurants, where visitors can discover one of the most representative desserts of Bogotá's sweet tradition.

Experience in Bogotá

Trying this dessert is a journey back in time that evokes Bogotá afternoons of the past, ideal for sharing with family while enjoying the capital's cool landscape.

TRADITIONAL DESSERT

Cuajada con Melao

Cuajada con melao is a perfect combination of traditional Colombian textures and flavors. It consists of a portion of fresh and soft curd cheese generously covered with a thick, hot syrup made from panela, creating a contrast between sweetness and softness.

This traditional dessert is best enjoyed in local eateries, traditional restaurants in Usaquén, and the city's market squares, where it continues to be part of everyday gastronomy.

Experience in Bogotá

It is the perfect ending to a hearty weekend lunch, a simple and traditional combination that balances the palate with its comforting contrast of temperature and textures.

TRADITIONAL DESSERT

Brevas con Arequipe

Brevas con arequipe is a classic that is always present on the tables of Bogotá families. Figs are cooked in syrup until soft, then opened and filled with thick, creamy arequipe, creating a sweet combination characteristic of local confectionery.

This preparation can be found in bakeries, candy shops, and traditional restaurants in the capital, where it remains a popular choice for those looking for traditional flavors.

Experience in Bogotá

This sweet represents the traditional sweetness of Bogotá, a classic treat that turns any walk through traditional neighborhoods into a moment filled with nostalgia, flavor, and warmth.
